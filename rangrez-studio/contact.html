CLIENT ASSISTANCE

Frequently Answered Queries

Everything you need to know about our handloom heritage, bespoke fits, and international delivery.

Yes, Rangrez Studio ships across North America, the United Kingdom, Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Australasia via DHL Express and FedEx Insured Courier. International delivery typically arrives within 5 to 7 business days. All duties and customs are prepaid where indicated.
Every garment crafted by Rangrez Studio features generous 2-inch concealed seam margins. If you require minor bodice tapering, sleeve adjustments, or length hem alterations, bring your piece to our Mehrauli or Kala Ghoda flagship salons for complimentary tailoring within 60 days of purchase.
For our festive and bridal couture edits (such as the Sultana Lehenga or Zafira Velvet Cape), we offer custom dye-lot formulations from our mineral earth archive and bespoke neckline variations. Please book an atelier consultation to discuss custom commissions with our design directors.
We offer seamless size exchanges within 14 days of delivery for all standard catalogue orders in original, unworn condition with atelier tags intact. Custom-tailored and bespoke monogrammed silhouettes are handcrafted to order and are considered final sales.
