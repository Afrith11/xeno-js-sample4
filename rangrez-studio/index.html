HERITAGE OF WEAVING

EVERY THREAD TELLS A STORY.

Indian textile artistry is not simply fabric; it is an oral history spoken in warp, weft, and natural mineral dyes.

At Rangrez Studio, our design ethos begins at the wooden pit looms of Maheshwar and the master zardozi frames of Shahpur Jat. We examine the subtle tactility of hand-spun tussar silk, the crystalline gossamer of Chanderi, and the geometric precision of kadwa Banarasi brocades. By preserving these time-honoured techniques in clean, asymmetric modern cuts, we create garments that transcend ephemeral fashion cycles.

14 Days to handweave each Chanderi panel
120+ Hours of intricate bullion zardozi needlework

THE MANIFESTO

TRADITION IS NOT STATIC.
IT EVOLVES.

Rangrez Studio was founded on an unapologetic belief: Indian ethnic dressing does not require Western validation to feel modern, nor does it require antiquated nostalgia to remain authentic.

We look at our historical costumes — the sweeping angrakhas of Awadh, the asymmetric drapes of the Konkan coast, the crisp kalis of Rajasthan — not as immutable dogma, but as an inexhaustible library of spatial design. We engineer garments that breathe in Mumbai humidity, move with New York pacing, and command reverence in London ballrooms.

— Meera Rangnekar, Creative Director
