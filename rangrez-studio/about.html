CHAPTER 01

The Generational Loom

Before a garment exists on paper, it begins as silk thread on wooden shuttle looms. In historical weaver clusters across Varanasi, Maheshwar, and Chanderi, our craft partnerships sustain ancient rhythmic methods that machines can never replicate.

We insist on genuine spun tussar, katan mulberry silks, and unbleached hand-spun cotton. In a world saturated with synthetic polyester imitations, our clothes retain the raw soul, slight textural irregularities, and breathing qualities of authentic Indian handlooms.

CHAPTER 02

The Mineral Earth Palette

The word Rangrez historically honours the master dyer — the artisan who possessed the alchemical intuition to transform water, bark, and dried petals into immortal colour.

Our signature seasonal hues — burnt terracotta, deep sindoor maroon, warm river sand, and fresh botanical marigold — are extracted from real madder root, harda, and temple marigold garlands. The result is a subdued luminescence that adapts to shifting atmospheric light from daylight to candlelit soirees.

CHAPTER 03

Deconstructed Drape

Traditional Indian clothing was never stitched; it was draped. We honour this fundamental fluidity while re-engineering it for modern mobility.

Our pattern masters deconstruct the six yards into pre-stitched origami folds that secure with invisible silk fasteners in sixty seconds. Kurtas adopt the sharp lines of Savile Row trench coats, while lehengas are weighted with micro-tulle underskirts that twirl effortlessly without crushing.

Atelier Milestones

Key moments in our evolution from a handloom experimental studio to an international luxury label.

2019

The First Handloom

Creative Director Meera Rangnekar collaborates with four weaver families in Chanderi to create the debut capsule of pure silk tunics.

2021

The Contemporary Cut

Launch of our signature pre-draped saree collection and architectural co-ords, garnering critical acclaim at India Fashion Week.

2024

Mehrauli Flagship

Inauguration of our flagship heritage atelier in New Delhi's historic Qutub quarter, offering bespoke couture appointments.

2026

The New India Edit

Global debut celebrating bold terracotta and maroon silhouettes on international runways and online boutique worldwide delivery.
